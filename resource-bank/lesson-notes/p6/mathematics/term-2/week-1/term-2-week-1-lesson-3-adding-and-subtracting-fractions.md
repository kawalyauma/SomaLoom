## Understand
The least common denominator (LCD) is the smallest positive number divisible by every denominator. Convert all fractions to this denominator before combining them.

## Worked examples
### Finding the LCD
For 1/6 + 1/4 − 1/3, the LCD of 6, 4 and 3 is 12.

### Combining three fractions
1/6 + 1/4 − 1/3 = 2/12 + 3/12 − 4/12 = 1/12.

### Check a subtraction
3/4 − 1/6 + 1/3 = 9/12 − 2/12 + 4/12 = 11/12.
