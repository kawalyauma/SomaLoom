## Understand
Division asks how many groups fit into a quantity. To divide by a non-zero fraction, multiply by its reciprocal. The reciprocal of a/b is b/a.

## Worked examples
### Fraction by fraction
3/4 ÷ 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/1 = 3/2 = 1 1/2.

### Whole by fraction
3 ÷ 2/5 = 3 × 5/2 = 15/2 = 7 1/2.

### Simplify
5/6 ÷ 10/9 = 5/6 × 9/10 = 45/60 = 3/4.
