## Subtraction of fractions

## Understand
Subtract fractions that refer to the same whole. A common denominator means the parts are the same size. If necessary, exchange one whole for a fraction before subtracting.

## Worked examples
### Same denominators
7/9 − 2/9 = (7 − 2)/9 = 5/9.

### Different denominators
5/6 − 1/4. Convert to twelfths: 10/12 − 3/12 = 7/12.

### Borrowing with mixed numbers
3 1/4 − 1 3/4: exchange one whole, 3 1/4 = 2 5/4. Then 2 5/4 − 1 3/4 = 1 2/4 = 1 1/2.
