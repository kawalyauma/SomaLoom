# P6 Mathematics — Term Two
## Addition of fractions: Teacher answer key

## Worked examples
### Same denominators
Add 3/8 + 2/8. The pieces are eighths, so add the numerators: (3 + 2)/8 = 5/8. Keep 8 as the denominator.

### Different denominators
Add 2/3 + 1/4. The lowest common denominator of 3 and 4 is 12. Therefore 2/3 = 8/12 and 1/4 = 3/12. The sum is 11/12.

### Mixed numbers
Add 1 2/3 + 2 3/4. Whole parts: 1 + 2 = 3. Fraction parts: 8/12 + 9/12 = 17/12 = 1 5/12. Total = 4 5/12.
