## Understand
Keep the order of operations and simplify exactly. Convert mixed numbers before multiplication or division, and use a common denominator for addition or subtraction.

## Worked examples
### Brackets and division
(1/2 + 1/4) ÷ 3/8 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 2.

### Multiply before subtract
5/6 − 1/2 × 1/3 = 5/6 − 1/6 = 2/3.

### Mixed fraction
1 1/2 + 2/3 ÷ 4/3 = 3/2 + 1/2 = 2.
