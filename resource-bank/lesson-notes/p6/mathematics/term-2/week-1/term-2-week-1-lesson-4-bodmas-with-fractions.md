## Understand
BODMAS means brackets, orders, division and multiplication, addition and subtraction. Multiplication and division share priority and are performed from left to right; addition and subtraction also share priority.

## Worked examples
### Brackets first
(1/2 + 1/4) × 2/3 = 3/4 × 2/3 = 1/2.

### Multiplication before addition
1/2 + 1/3 × 3/4 = 1/2 + 1/4 = 3/4.

### Left-to-right for equal priority
3/4 ÷ 1/2 × 2/3 = 3/2 × 2/3 = 1.
