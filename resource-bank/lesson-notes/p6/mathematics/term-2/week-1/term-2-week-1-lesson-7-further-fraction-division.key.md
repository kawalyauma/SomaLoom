# P6 Mathematics — Term Two
## Further fraction division: Teacher answer key

## Worked examples
### Mixed number by whole
2 1/4 ÷ 3 = 9/4 × 1/3 = 3/4.

### Mixed number by mixed number
2 1/2 ÷ 1 1/4 = 5/2 × 4/5 = 2.

### Fraction by mixed number
3/4 ÷ 1 1/2 = 3/4 × 2/3 = 1/2.
