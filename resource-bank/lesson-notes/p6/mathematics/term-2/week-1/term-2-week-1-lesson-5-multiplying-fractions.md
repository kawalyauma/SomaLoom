## Multiplying fractions
Name: _____________________  Date: ____________

## Understand
Multiplying by a fraction finds a part of a quantity. Multiply numerators together and denominators together; cancel common factors to simplify.

## Worked examples
### Fraction of a whole number
3/5 of 20 = 3/5 × 20 = 60/5 = 12.

### Two fractions
2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2.

### Mixed number
1 1/2 × 2/3 = 3/2 × 2/3 = 1.
