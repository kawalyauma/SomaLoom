# Primary Six Mathematics — Finding unknown prime factors

## Teacher answer key

## Worked examples

1. **Two numbers are 12 and x. Their HCF is 6 and LCM 60. Find x.**
   - 6×60=12×x; x=30. Check: HCF(12,30)=6 and LCM(12,30)=60.
2. **If 36=2²×3², which prime factor is missing in 2×2×3×__?**
   - 3.

## Practice answers

1. **Find x if HCF=3, LCM=30, a=6.** — x = (3 × 30) ÷ 6 = 15. Check: HCF(6,15)=3 and LCM(6,15)=30. Both conditions hold.
2. **Find x if HCF=5, LCM=60, a=15.** — x = (5 × 60) ÷ 15 = 20. Check: HCF(15,20)=5; LCM(15,20)=60.
3. **Missing factor: 30=2×3×__.** — 5
4. **Missing factor: 84=2×2×3×__.** — 7
5. **HCF of 18 and 27.** — 9
6. **LCM of 18 and 27.** — 54
7. **HCF×LCM for 8 and 12.** — 96
8. **Find b if HCF=4, LCM=40, a=8.** — b = (4 × 40) ÷ 8 = 20. Check: HCF(8,20)=4; LCM(8,20)=40.

Marking: Accept correct equivalent methods and simplified answers.
