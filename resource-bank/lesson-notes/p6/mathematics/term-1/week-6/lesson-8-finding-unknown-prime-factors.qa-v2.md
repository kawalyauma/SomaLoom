# Primary Six Mathematics — Finding unknown prime factors

Name: ____________________  Date: __________

## Learn and understand

Use factor trees and shared prime factors. For positive whole numbers a and b, HCF(a,b) × LCM(a,b) = a × b. After calculating an unknown number, always verify BOTH its HCF and LCM with the known number.

## Worked examples

**Example 1:** Two numbers are 12 and x. Their HCF is 6 and LCM 60. Find x.

**Working:** 6×60=12×x; x=30.

**Example 2:** If 36=2²×3², which prime factor is missing in 2×2×3×__?

**Working:** 3.
