# Teacher key — Investigating pitch and loudness

## Scientific guidance
A vibrating ruler behaves approximately as a cantilever. Under otherwise similar conditions, shortening the free length increases its vibration frequency, giving a higher pitch. Larger plucking displacement generally increases amplitude and loudness. It does not by itself guarantee a higher pitch. The experiment is qualitative; do not invent exact frequencies or loudness values. Use a flexible ruler, gentle plucks and adult supervision.
