# Teacher Answer Key — P6 IRE
## Surat al-Fil: Lessons for Daily Life

### Suggested answers
1. Humility means not thinking you are better than others and treating people respectfully.
2. Share materials, listen to others, avoid boasting, help a pupil who struggles.
3. Pray or seek Allah's guidance while also making wise efforts and seeking appropriate help.
4. Keep the pupil safe if possible, do not join in, report the bullying to a trusted teacher/adult.
5. It harms other people and their property, disrespects worship, and may cause fear or conflict.
6. Strength does not give the right to control or hurt others; show humility and kindness.
7. Listen calmly, talk respectfully, seek mediation from a teacher/elder, apologise when wrong.
8. Review mistakes; ask the teacher for help; plan revision; practise difficult questions; pray for guidance.
9. Examples: bullying, vandalism, spreading harmful rumours, threatening others.
10. Accept a practical respectful action, e.g. listening, speaking kindly, respecting worship and property.

### Assessing application
Look for specific safe actions, not merely a repeated slogan. Do not imply that every bad event is punishment from Allah or that a pupil is to blame for being harmed. Reward thoughtful, peaceful decisions and respect for people of other faiths. The personal action plan is reflective and should not be graded on disclosure of private experiences.

### Extension discussion
Ask pupils to explain why seeking adult help during bullying is responsible rather than cowardly. The surah teaches humility and trust; it must not be used to justify violence against any group.
