### What you will learn
- Identify Surat al-Fil as Chapter 105 of the Qur'an.
- Recite its five verses with the guidance of a knowledgeable teacher.
- Practise memorising the verses in the correct order.
- Explain why careful listening and respectful recitation matter.

### About the surah
**Surat al-Fil** means **The Elephant**. It is Chapter 105 of the Qur'an and has **five verses**. The surah tells of the people of the elephant who came against the Ka'bah in Makkah. Allah protected the sacred place. We learn to avoid pride and to trust Allah while doing what is right.

### A good way to learn a short surah
1. Open the Qur'an at Chapter 105. Follow the written Arabic as the teacher recites.
2. Listen to the first verse without speaking. Notice each sound.
3. Repeat the verse slowly after the teacher. Ask for help with difficult sounds.
4. Practise the verse again, then move to the next verse.
5. Join the verses in their correct order. Check against the Qur'an.
6. Recite from memory and ask the teacher to correct any mistake.

**Remember:** Do not guess the Arabic words from English. Use the Qur'an and a qualified teacher or reliable recording for the exact wording and pronunciation.

### Vocabulary for understanding
- **Surah:** a chapter of the Qur'an.
- **Verse (ayah):** a numbered part of a surah.
- **Ka'bah:** the sacred building in Makkah towards which Muslims face in prayer.
- **Recitation:** reading the Qur'an aloud carefully.
- **Memorisation:** learning words so that you can recall them without looking.
