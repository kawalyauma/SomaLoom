# Teacher Answer Key — P6 IRE
## Surat al-Fil: Recitation and Memorisation

### Learning and assessment
Use an accurate printed Qur'an (Chapter 105) and a qualified reciter/teacher for Arabic pronunciation. This resource intentionally does not print unverified Arabic or transliteration. Check each verse in sequence; assess respectful listening, articulation, recall and willingness to correct mistakes. Do not award automatic full marks from written work alone.

### Suggested answers
1. The Elephant.
2. Chapter 105.
3. Five verses.
4. Any two: listen carefully; repeat slowly; read from the Qur'an; practise small sections; check with a teacher; revise regularly.
5. To avoid changing or guessing the words of the Qur'an and to learn correct recitation.
6. Reading the Qur'an aloud carefully.
7. Ask the teacher for help, listen again and practise until corrected.
8. Listen, then repeat, then recite from memory, then check (checking may also happen after each attempt).
9. Performance assessment: five verses in correct order with pronunciation checked against the Qur'an by a competent teacher. Record individual feedback; no written-only answer.
10. Examples: humility, trust in Allah, avoiding harm or arrogance, respect for sacred places.

### Oral assessment record
| Criterion | Evidence to record |
|---|---|
| Order of five verses | Correct / developing / needs support |
| Pronunciation | Teacher-verified / needs support |
| Recall | Independent / with prompts / not yet |
| Respectful listening | Observed / needs support |

### Misconception checks
- The title refers to the elephant, not to a sixth verse.
- The Qur'an text should be consulted for recitation; English paraphrases are not Qur'anic Arabic.
- Do not treat a pupil's imperfect pronunciation as moral failure; provide patient correction.
