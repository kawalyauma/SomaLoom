# Teacher Answer Key — P6 IRE
## Surat al-Fil: Meaning and the Story of the Elephant

### Key content check
Qur'an Chapter 105 refers to the people of the elephant, their defeated plan, birds in flocks, stones of baked clay and the final comparison to eaten straw. The name Abraha comes from historical tradition, not the surah's five verses. Do not present additional story details as direct Qur'anic quotations.

### Suggested answers
1. The Elephant.
2. Makkah, especially the Ka'bah.
3. Allah.
4. Stones of baked clay.
5. Their attack failed; the army was defeated and described as being like eaten straw.
6. The five verses do not name Abraha. That name is supplied by traditional historical accounts of the event.
7. To enter a place by force to take control of it.
8. The people of the elephant approached Makkah; their plan was defeated; Allah sent birds with stones of baked clay; the attacking force was destroyed. Accept equivalent ordering that follows Qur'an 105.
9. Strength does not give anyone the right to harm others; avoid arrogance and bullying.
10. Examples: respect others' worship, report threats to responsible adults, keep the place clean, resolve disagreements peacefully, protect property without violence.

### Assessment and safeguarding notes
Accept pupils' own short wording if accurate. Do not ask pupils to act out harm against others. Emphasise that the story does not justify hostility towards people of different religions. For oral retelling, check that a pupil does not attribute the traditional name Abraha to the Qur'an itself.
