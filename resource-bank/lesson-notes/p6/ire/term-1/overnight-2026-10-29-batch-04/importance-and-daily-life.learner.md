# Primary Six Islamic Religious Education
## Surat al-Fil: Lessons for Daily Life

Name: __________________________  Date: __________________

### What you will learn
- Identify values Muslims learn from Surat al-Fil.
- Apply the surah's message to situations at home, school and in the community.
- Choose peaceful and responsible actions when people are threatened.
- Explain why trust in Allah goes together with doing what is right.

### Important lessons
**Humility:** Do not boast about strength, money, marks or popularity. Treat others with respect.

**Trust in Allah:** Muslims believe Allah has power over creation. They seek His help while also making wise and responsible choices.

**Avoiding harm:** Do not join plans to hurt people, damage property or threaten a place of worship.

**Courage to do good:** Speak to a trusted adult when someone is being bullied or a shared place is in danger. Do not respond with violence.

### Three school situations
**Situation A — Bullying:** A group of older pupils threatens a younger pupil. A good response is to move the younger pupil to safety and tell a teacher. Do not join the group.

**Situation B — Respect for worship:** Pupils pass a mosque or church. They should avoid shouting insults or damaging anything. People can disagree about religion and still respect one another.

**Situation C — A difficult day:** A pupil fails a test. The pupil can pray, ask for guidance, review mistakes and study again. Trust does not mean refusing to make an effort.

### A decision path
**Notice a problem**; then **choose a peaceful action**; **ask for help if needed**; **protect people and property**.
