# TEACHER KEY — OBJECTIVES AND BENEFITS OF EAC COOPERATION

## Learning checks
Treat integration pillars as programmes or goals with differing implementation status. The new EAC Treaty was signed on 30 November 1999 and entered into force on 7 July 2000. The 1993 tripartite cooperation agreement was a step towards revival, not the new EAC's legal start date.

## Worked examples
Example 1: Trade cooperation helps traders reach wider markets.
Example 2: Health cooperation helps countries respond to cross-border disease risks.

## Answers

4. **2000.**
5. Trade in goods between member countries, with coordinated external customs duties.
6. Movement of goods, services, people/workers and investment under agreed rules.
7. Customs Union, Common Market, Monetary Union, Political Federation.
8. No. A single currency is a planned goal of monetary integration, not an already adopted currency for all partner countries.
9. No. The partner countries remain separate states; political federation is a long-term goal.
10. Access to a larger market, improved transport, or simpler agreed trade arrangements.
11. Examples: educational cooperation; safer travel; health programmes; improved communication.
12. An objective is a desired result; an achievement is a result already attained.

Countries can agree on pollution controls and protect fish and water used by communities on both sides of the border.

## Marking guidance
Accept accurate equivalent examples. Avoid claiming that every promised freedom or programme is fully implemented.
