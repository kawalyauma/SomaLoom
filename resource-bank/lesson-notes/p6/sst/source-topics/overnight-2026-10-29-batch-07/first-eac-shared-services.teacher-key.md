# TEACHER KEY — SHARED SERVICES OF THE FIRST EAC

## Learning checks
The first EAC began in 1967 and ended in 1977. Founders: Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania. Historical services must not be presented as identical to the revived EAC's current institutions.

## Worked examples
Example 1: Railways and harbours carried goods by train and handled cargo at ports.
Example 2: Posts carried letters and parcels between places.

## Answers
1. **1967.**
2. **Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania.**
3. **1977.**
4. A service organised for the benefit of more than one country.
5. Any two: railways, harbours or shipping, air transport.
6. Trains moved goods and harbours handled cargo, helping traders reach markets.
7. Uganda is landlocked; access to seaports through neighbours supports overseas trade.
8. Postal services (posts).
9. Telecommunications.
10. Cooperation helped countries coordinate customs duties and border trade procedures.
11. Any supported example: examinations, educational publications, or scientific research.
12. The first EAC existed from 1967 to 1977 and operated certain joint services; the revived EAC began in 2000 with newer institutions and integration programmes.

## Think further — sample
A farmer may use transport to take bananas to a market and communication services to contact a buyer. Accept other plausible services with explanation.

## Marking guidance
Accept accurate equivalent wording. Do not require pupils to memorise unverified old abbreviations. Distinguish historical from current services.
