## Meaning of an objective
An **objective** is something people plan to achieve. Countries cooperate when they agree to work towards shared objectives.

The East African Community (EAC) first began in 1967. That organisation ended in 1977. Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania signed a new EAC Treaty in 1999, and the revived Community began in 2000.

## Why East African countries cooperate
**Trade:** Make it easier for people and businesses to buy and sell goods across borders.

**Transport and communication:** Improve links that help people, goods and information move between countries.

**Peace and security:** Encourage countries to solve disagreements peacefully and work together on shared problems.

**Development:** Support jobs, investment, education, health and better living conditions.

**Friendship and unity:** Encourage respect and cooperation among the peoples of East Africa.

## Four long-term areas of EAC integration
The revived EAC describes four main pillars: **Customs Union, Common Market, Monetary Union and Political Federation**.

[Diagram: Four pillars of East African cooperation]

A **Customs Union** aims to make trade in goods easier and use a common approach to duties on goods from outside the region.

A **Common Market** aims to make it easier for goods, services, workers and investment to move across partner countries, under agreed rules.

A **Monetary Union** is a plan for closer cooperation on money and currencies. **Do not say that all EAC countries already use one currency.**

A **Political Federation** is a long-term goal of closer political unity. **Do not say that the EAC is already one country.**

## Benefits in daily life
- A trader may reach more customers in neighbouring countries.
- A learner may benefit when countries cooperate in education.
- Better transport links may reduce the time needed to move goods.
- Cooperation can help countries respond to shared health and environmental problems.

A **goal** is not the same as a **completed achievement**. Some EAC plans take many years to put into practice.

## Worked examples
**Example 1:** A trader wants to sell maize across a border. Which objective is relevant? **Improving trade**, because the trader needs access to another market.

**Example 2:** Two countries discuss how to stop a disease spreading across their border. Which objective is relevant? **Cooperation in health and development**, because the problem affects both countries.
