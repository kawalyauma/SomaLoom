# PRIMARY SIX CRE — TEACHER ANSWER KEY
## Our Response to Suffering

**Curriculum topic:** Evil and Suffering
**Subtopic:** Our response to suffering

### Teaching focus
Assess understanding, practical judgement and respectful language. Award credit for other accurate, age-appropriate answers. Do not require learners to disclose personal trauma.

### Bible accuracy notes
- Job 1–2
- Job 42
- Luke 10:25–37

### Checked practice answers

1. **List four possible causes of suffering.**
   **Answer:** Illness, accidents, violence, poverty, drought, flooding, bereavement; any four.

2. **Explain why it is unfair to blame every sick person for their illness.**
   **Answer:** Illness can have many causes; suffering is not proof of wrongdoing.

3. **Name two lessons from the story of Job.**
   **Answer:** People can face hardship without being at fault; avoid hasty judgement; listen and persevere; any two.

4. **Give three helpful responses when someone has suffered a loss.**
   **Answer:** Listen, comfort, seek responsible adult support, share appropriate items, help with safe practical needs; any three.

5. **Give two harmful responses that should be avoided.**
   **Answer:** Mocking, blame, revenge, rumours, concealing abuse, refusing necessary care; any two.

6. **What should a pupil do when a friend reports bullying or abuse?**
   **Answer:** Listen without blaming and tell a trusted responsible adult promptly; do not promise secrecy about danger.

7. **Why is prayer alone not enough when a person needs urgent medical care?**
   **Answer:** Prayer may provide comfort but urgent medical problems need trained help and appropriate treatment.

8. **Write two ways pupils can reduce avoidable suffering at school.**
   **Answer:** Follow safety rules, prevent bullying, practise hygiene, report dangers, resolve conflict peacefully; any two.

9. **A family has lost its home after flooding. Suggest three safe forms of support.**
   **Answer:** Help collect approved food or clothing, support an organised school drive, send a respectful message, alert responsible adults; any three.

10. **Complete the sentence: A responsible response to suffering should protect a person’s ______ and ______.**
   **Answer:** Life and dignity (accept safety and dignity, or health and dignity where clearly explained).

### Assessment and safeguarding
- Mark content and practical reasoning; do not reward unsafe actions.
- If a pupil discloses harm or abuse, follow school safeguarding procedures rather than discussing details in front of the class.
