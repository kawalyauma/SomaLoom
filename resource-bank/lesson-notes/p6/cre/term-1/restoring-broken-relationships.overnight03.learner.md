### What is a broken relationship?

- A relationship is a connection between people, such as family members, friends and neighbours. Christians also speak about a relationship between people and God.
- A relationship becomes damaged when trust, care or respect is lost. Dishonesty, cruelty, jealousy, broken promises and refusal to listen may cause conflict.
- Restoration means taking honest steps to repair what has been damaged. It may take time.

### Bible examples of damaged relationships

- Genesis 3 tells how Adam and Eve disobeyed God. In Christian teaching, disobedience damaged the relationship between humanity and God.
- Genesis 4:1–10 tells of Cain’s jealousy and violence towards his brother Abel. This shows how unchecked anger can destroy family relationships.
- 1 John 3:11–12 teaches Christians to love one another and warns against following Cain’s harmful example.
- Christians believe God calls people to repentance, forgiveness and renewed life. A redeemer is someone who rescues or restores; Christians believe Jesus is their redeemer.

### Steps towards making peace

- 1. Stop harmful words or actions. Calm down before speaking.
- 2. Admit what you did wrong without blaming another person.
- 3. Offer a sincere apology and listen to how the other person feels.
- 4. Repair the harm where possible: return borrowed property, correct a lie or replace what you damaged.
- 5. Show change through honest behaviour. Trust is rebuilt over time.

### Forgiveness and safety

- Forgiveness means choosing not to seek revenge. It does not mean pretending harm never happened.
- A person who has been hurt may need time and help from a trusted adult. No pupil should be forced to stay in an unsafe situation.
- When bullying, abuse or serious threats occur, report them to a responsible adult. Safety comes before attempts at reconciliation.

### A school example

- Moses borrows Ruth’s ruler and breaks it. He first hides the damage. To restore trust, he should admit the truth, apologise and arrange a replacement with an adult if needed.
- Ruth may forgive him but still expect him to be more careful. The relationship improves when his future actions match his apology.

### Remember
Kind words are important, but safe actions and respect make them meaningful.
