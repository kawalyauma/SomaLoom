### What you will learn
- Explain our response to suffering.
- Use a Bible example to make a responsible choice in everyday life.

### What is a responsible response?

- People may suffer because of illness, accidents, violence, poverty, drought or loss. Not every person who suffers has done something wrong.
- A responsible response protects life and dignity. It includes seeking help, caring for others and making safe choices.
- Christians are encouraged to show hope, patience, prayer and practical care. Prayer does not replace medical help or other necessary action.

### Job: facing suffering without giving up

- The book of Job tells of a man who faced loss and severe pain. His story shows that suffering cannot always be explained by blaming the person who suffers.
- Job struggled, asked questions and continued to seek God. His friends’ quick judgements were not a helpful way to comfort him.
- We should listen before giving advice. We should not tell a sick person that their illness proves they are sinful.

### Helpful responses and harmful responses

- Helpful: tell a trusted adult, get suitable care, listen, comfort, share responsibly and look for safe solutions.
- Harmful: mocking, blaming, revenge, spreading rumours, refusing treatment or encouraging someone to hide abuse.
- When a pupil is bullied or abused, the right response is to seek help from a trusted teacher, parent, guardian or appropriate protection service.

### Preventing avoidable suffering

- Follow road-safety rules and avoid risky play.
- Practise hygiene and use clean drinking water.
- Respect other people and settle disagreements without violence.
- Report dangerous situations and unfair treatment to a responsible adult.
- Use money and shared resources honestly. Do not take part in cheating or intimidation.

### A classroom situation

- After heavy rain, a family’s home is damaged. Pupils can collect agreed useful items through the school, avoid spreading rumours and let responsible adults organise safe assistance.
- They should not enter a dangerous building or take pictures of the family without permission.

### Remember
Kind words are important, but safe actions and respect make them meaningful.
