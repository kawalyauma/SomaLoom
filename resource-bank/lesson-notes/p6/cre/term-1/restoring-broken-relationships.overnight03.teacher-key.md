# PRIMARY SIX CRE — TEACHER ANSWER KEY
## Restoring Broken Relationships

**Curriculum topic:** God Calls His People
**Subtopic:** Restoration of broken relationships

### Teaching focus
Assess understanding, practical judgement and respectful language. Award credit for other accurate, age-appropriate answers. Do not require learners to disclose personal trauma.

### Bible accuracy notes
- Genesis 3
- Genesis 4:1–10
- 1 John 3:11–12

### Checked practice answers

1. **Define a broken relationship.**
   **Answer:** A relationship in which trust, respect or care has been damaged.

2. **Give three actions that can damage trust between friends.**
   **Answer:** Lying, insults, jealousy, breaking promises, taking belongings, violence; any three.

3. **What caused conflict between Cain and Abel?**
   **Answer:** Cain was jealous/angry and killed his brother Abel; the lesson concerns harmful jealousy and violence.

4. **What lesson about loving others is taught in 1 John 3:11–12?**
   **Answer:** Love one another and do not follow Cain’s example of hatred and violence.

5. **Explain repentance in simple words.**
   **Answer:** Turning away from wrongdoing, admitting it and choosing to change.

6. **List four steps that can help restore a damaged friendship.**
   **Answer:** Stop harmful action; admit wrong; apologise; listen; make amends; demonstrate change; any four.

7. **Why is saying “sorry” not always enough?**
   **Answer:** The person must also repair harm and change behaviour; trust may take time to return.

8. **Explain why forgiveness does not mean staying in danger.**
   **Answer:** Forgiveness is not permission for continued abuse; seek safety and responsible adult help.

9. **What should Moses do after breaking Ruth’s ruler?**
   **Answer:** Tell the truth, apologise and arrange to replace or repair the ruler.

10. **Write a short apology that accepts responsibility without blaming someone else.**
   **Answer:** Example: “I broke your ruler and hid it. That was wrong. I am sorry. I will replace it and tell the truth next time.” Accept any sincere accountable wording.

### Assessment and safeguarding
- Mark content and practical reasoning; do not reward unsafe actions.
- If a pupil discloses harm or abuse, follow school safeguarding procedures rather than discussing details in front of the class.
