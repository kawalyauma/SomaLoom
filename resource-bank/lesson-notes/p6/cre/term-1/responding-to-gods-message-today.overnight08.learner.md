### How Christians learn God’s message

Christians read and study the Bible to learn about God and how to live. They also learn through prayer, worship, teaching and discussion in their church communities.

Christians believe that Jesus taught people to love God and their neighbours. They try to show that love through truthfulness, fairness, forgiveness and practical care.

### What a responsible response looks like

A responsible response is more than listening. It means choosing actions that match what has been learnt. For example, a person who values honesty does not hide a mistake or take another person’s property.

Repentance means admitting wrong and changing behaviour. Forgiveness means choosing not to take revenge. Service means helping others without demanding a reward.

### Examples at school and at home

At school, learners can include a lonely classmate, return lost property, speak truthfully and stop harmful teasing. At home, they can help with safe tasks and show respect to others.

In the community, Christians may support people who are sick, care for shared water sources, help those in need and work peacefully with neighbours of different beliefs.

### How to judge a claimed message safely

Not every statement made in God’s name should be accepted without thought. Christians can compare a claim with the Bible’s teaching about love, truth and justice. They can ask a responsible adult or trusted religious leader for guidance.

No child should follow instructions to hide abuse, give away family money secretly, hurt another person or refuse necessary medical help. Safety and honesty matter.

### Make a thoughtful choice

When a difficult situation arises: listen carefully, check what is true, consider whether the action is kind and safe, seek good advice and choose a responsible response.

People may disagree respectfully about religious questions. A Christian can practise faith without insulting or forcing others to agree.
