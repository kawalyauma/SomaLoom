# PRIMARY SIX CRE — TEACHER ANSWER KEY
## Sharing in the Suffering of Others

**Curriculum topic:** Evil and Suffering
**Subtopic:** How to share in the suffering of others

### Teaching focus
Assess understanding, practical judgement and respectful language. Award credit for other accurate, age-appropriate answers. Do not require learners to disclose personal trauma.

### Bible accuracy notes
- Luke 10:25–37
- John 18:10–11

### Checked practice answers

1. **Explain the meaning of compassion in your own words.**
   **Answer:** Compassion is concern for a person in difficulty shown through kindness and useful help.

2. **Who stopped to help the injured traveller in Jesus’ story?**
   **Answer:** The Samaritan.

3. **State two practical things the Samaritan did.**
   **Answer:** He treated the wounds, transported the man to safety and arranged care; any two.

4. **Give three safe ways a pupil can help a classmate who is suffering.**
   **Answer:** Listen kindly; include the pupil; share suitable materials; ask a teacher for help; give a kind message; any three safe actions.

5. **Why should a pupil tell an adult about a serious injury?**
   **Answer:** An adult can arrange appropriate first aid or professional care; pupils should not attempt unsafe treatment.

6. **Why is it wrong to spread stories about another person’s illness?**
   **Answer:** It violates privacy and dignity and may cause stigma or distress.

7. **A new pupil is sitting alone at break. Write two kind actions you could take.**
   **Answer:** Invite the pupil to join a game and introduce them to friendly classmates; other suitable actions accepted.

8. **How is helping a stranger different from helping only your friends?**
   **Answer:** Helping a stranger shows that compassion is not limited to people we already know.

9. **What should you do if someone asks you to keep a dangerous injury secret?**
   **Answer:** Tell a trusted adult immediately even if someone asks you to keep it secret.

10. **Write a four-step plan for helping an elderly neighbour, with adult guidance.**
   **Answer:** For example: ask an adult about a safe need; plan a suitable visit; bring an agreed item or offer a simple chore with supervision; follow up respectfully.

### Assessment and safeguarding
- Mark content and practical reasoning; do not reward unsafe actions.
- If a pupil discloses harm or abuse, follow school safeguarding procedures rather than discussing details in front of the class.
