# PRIMARY SIX CRE — TEACHER ANSWER KEY
## God’s Message for His People Today

**Curriculum topic:** God Calls His People
**Subtopic:** God’s message for his people today

### Teaching focus
Mark for accurate understanding, application and respectful reasoning. Accept other well-supported equivalent answers.

### Checked practice questions and answers

1. **Name two ways Christians learn about God’s message.**
   **Answer:** Bible reading, prayer, worship, church teaching or discussion; any two.

2. **What does repentance mean?**
   **Answer:** Recognising wrongdoing, turning away from it and changing behaviour.

3. **What is forgiveness?**
   **Answer:** Choosing not to seek revenge; it does not require accepting further harm.

4. **Give three ways a P6 pupil can show care at school.**
   **Answer:** Include a lonely pupil, help appropriately, avoid bullying, return property, speak kindly; any three.

5. **Why are actions important as well as words?**
   **Answer:** They show whether a person really follows the values they say they believe.

6. **A friend is being bullied. What is a safe Christian response?**
   **Answer:** Support the friend, do not join bullying, report to a trusted adult; avoid unsafe confrontation.

7. **A stranger claims God told you to give them your family’s money secretly. What should you do?**
   **Answer:** Do not comply; tell a trusted adult and seek help. Religious claims do not excuse secrecy or exploitation.

8. **Give one example of caring for a shared community resource.**
   **Answer:** Keep a water source clean, avoid littering, report damage, conserve water; any one.

9. **Why should Christians respect neighbours of other beliefs?**
   **Answer:** People deserve dignity and peaceful treatment; love of neighbour does not require agreement on religion.

10. **Write four steps for making a wise decision when a message seems doubtful.**
   **Answer:** Listen; check facts and values; seek trusted advice; choose a safe, honest response. Accept equivalent sequence.

### Scripture and teaching checks

- This lesson is the NCDC CRE Term I subtopic “God’s message for his people today”, not a claim of fresh prophecy.
- Link to John the Baptist’s practical ethical demands (Luke 3:10–14) and Mary’s responsible response (Luke 1:26–38).
- Respect different Christian traditions and pupils from other religions; do not require pupils to share private religious experiences.
- Safeguarding: never imply obedience to religious authority overrides child protection or medical care.

### Assessment and safeguarding
- Do not require children to disclose private experiences or repeat harmful religious claims.
- If a child discloses danger, follow school safeguarding procedures.
