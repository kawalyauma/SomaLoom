# PRIMARY SIX CRE — TEACHER ANSWER KEY
## Bible Characters Who Received Messages from God

**Curriculum topic:** God Calls His People
**Subtopic:** Bible characters who received messages from God

### Teaching focus
Mark for accurate understanding, application and respectful reasoning. Accept other well-supported equivalent answers.

### Checked practice questions and answers

1. **What is a message?**
   **Answer:** A message is information or an instruction communicated to someone.

2. **What is a messenger?**
   **Answer:** A messenger is a person who carries or communicates a message.

3. **What did God ask Abram to do in Genesis 12?**
   **Answer:** Leave his homeland and travel to the land God would show him.

4. **Give one action that showed Abram’s response.**
   **Answer:** He set out with his household; he travelled as directed; he built altars. Any one.

5. **Where did Moses encounter God, and what task was he given?**
   **Answer:** At the burning bush; he was to go to Pharaoh and lead the Israelites out of slavery.

6. **Why did the Israelites first struggle to listen to Moses in Exodus 6?**
   **Answer:** They were distressed by harsh slavery and a broken spirit.

7. **Why was Jeremiah unsure about being a messenger?**
   **Answer:** He thought he was too young and could not speak well.

8. **Name two different responses to God’s message shown in these stories.**
   **Answer:** Examples: Abram obeyed; Moses questioned; Jeremiah feared and was reassured; Israelites did not listen at first. Any two.

9. **A pupil hears an unverified message that school will close tomorrow. What should the pupil do?**
   **Answer:** Check with a teacher or school office; do not spread the rumour.

10. **Explain one lesson a P6 pupil can learn from these Bible characters.**
   **Answer:** Possible: be truthful when carrying messages; ask responsible questions; act courageously; seek safe adult guidance. Accept explained examples.

### Scripture and teaching checks

- Use Genesis 12:1–9, Exodus 3:1–13; 6:8–9 and Jeremiah 1:4–9 as the reference passages.
- Abram is called Abraham later in Genesis; do not imply the name change happens in Genesis 12.
- Do not present the Israelites’ first refusal as simple disobedience without their suffering context.
- A pupil should not be taught to obey unsafe instructions from someone claiming religious authority.

### Assessment and safeguarding
- Do not require children to disclose private experiences or repeat harmful religious claims.
- If a child discloses danger, follow school safeguarding procedures.
