# PRIMARY SIX CRE — TEACHER ANSWER KEY
## The Promise of Salvation: Mary and John the Baptist

**Curriculum topic:** God Calls His People
**Subtopic:** The promise of salvation

### Teaching focus
Mark for accurate understanding, application and respectful reasoning. Accept other well-supported equivalent answers.

### Checked practice questions and answers

1. **What does salvation mean in Christian teaching?**
   **Answer:** Rescue from sin and restoration of a good relationship with God through Jesus Christ.

2. **What is a redeemer?**
   **Answer:** Someone who rescues or brings freedom; Christians believe Jesus is their Redeemer.

3. **Who brought a message to Mary?**
   **Answer:** The angel Gabriel.

4. **Where was Mary living when she received the message?**
   **Answer:** Nazareth.

5. **What name was Mary told to give her son?**
   **Answer:** Jesus.

6. **How did Mary respond after asking a question?**
   **Answer:** She accepted the responsibility and expressed willingness to follow God’s plan.

7. **What did John the Baptist ask people to do?**
   **Answer:** Repent, turn away from wrongdoing and live fairly.

8. **Give two practical changes John encouraged.**
   **Answer:** Share clothing or food; collect only the proper amount of tax; avoid threats, false accusations or extortion. Any two.

9. **How were Gabriel’s message to Mary and John’s message to the crowds different?**
   **Answer:** Gabriel announced Jesus’ coming birth to Mary; John publicly called people to repentance and preparation.

10. **A pupil copied homework and then apologised. Name two actions that would show genuine repentance.**
   **Answer:** Admit what happened, complete future work honestly, accept appropriate consequences, seek help to learn; any two.

### Scripture and teaching checks

- Luke 1:26–38 is the annunciation to Mary; Luke 3:3–16 is John’s preaching and baptism.
- Luke 3:10–14 gives concrete examples of sharing, honest tax collection and non-extortion.
- Do not claim John baptised Jesus in Luke 3:3–16 specifically; the lesson concerns John’s public message.
- Do not conflate Gabriel’s announcement with John’s later preaching or claim both messages happened on the same day.

### Assessment and safeguarding
- Do not require children to disclose private experiences or repeat harmful religious claims.
- If a child discloses danger, follow school safeguarding procedures.
