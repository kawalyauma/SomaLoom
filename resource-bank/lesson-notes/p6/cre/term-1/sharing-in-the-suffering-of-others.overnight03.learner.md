### What does it mean to share in suffering?

- Suffering is pain, distress or difficulty experienced by a person. It may be physical, emotional or social.
- To share in another person’s suffering means to notice their need and give kind, practical support. We do not need to feel exactly the same pain to help.
- Compassion is caring about someone who is suffering and choosing to help.

### A Bible example: the Good Samaritan

- In Luke 10:25–37, Jesus tells a story about a traveller who was attacked and left injured beside the road.
- Two people passed without helping. A Samaritan stopped, treated the injured man, carried him to a safe place and arranged care.
- The Samaritan helped a stranger. Jesus used the story to teach that being a neighbour means showing mercy through action.

### Ways a Primary Six learner can help

- Listen kindly to a classmate who is worried. Do not laugh at their problem.
- Include a pupil who is lonely or has a disability in games and group work.
- Share suitable school materials when you can do so fairly.
- Visit or write a kind message to someone who is ill, with an adult’s guidance.
- Tell a trusted adult if a person is injured, threatened, neglected or seriously unwell. Do not attempt dangerous rescue or medical treatment yourself.
- Respect privacy: do not spread stories about someone’s illness or family difficulties.

### A simple way to act

- Notice the need. Ask politely whether help is wanted. Give safe help you can manage. Tell a responsible adult when necessary. Check later that the person is supported.
- Helping is not showing off. It is treating people with dignity, whatever their background or beliefs.

### Example from school

- A pupil falls on the playground and cannot stand. A good response is to keep the area clear, call a teacher immediately and speak calmly to the pupil.
- A poor response is to crowd around, laugh, move the injured pupil carelessly or post pictures of the injury.

### Remember
Kind words are important, but safe actions and respect make them meaningful.
