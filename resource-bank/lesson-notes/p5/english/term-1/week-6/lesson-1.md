## THE PRESENT SIMPLE PASSIVE

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the present simple passive.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
The present simple passive is used for regular actions and facts when the receiver is more important than the doer.

### Important rules
- Form: subject + am/is/are + past participle.
- Use is for a singular noun, are for a plural noun and am with I.
- Use am/is/are not for negative sentences; reverse the helping verb and subject for questions.

### Examples
1. The classroom is swept daily.
2. The desks are cleaned every Friday.
3. English is spoken in our school.
4. These books are borrowed often.
5. The floor is not washed on Sundays.
6. Are the gates locked at night?

### Worked example
**Question:** Make passive: The cook prepares lunch every day.

**Answer:** Lunch is prepared by the cook every day.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| The gate are locked. | The gate is locked. |
| The desks is polished. | The desks are polished. |
| The room is sweep. | The room is swept. |
| The food are cooked. | The food is cooked. |
