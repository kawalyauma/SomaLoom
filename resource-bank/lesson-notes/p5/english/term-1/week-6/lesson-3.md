## PREFER ... TO AND LIKE ... MORE THAN

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of prefer ... to and like ... more than.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
We can express a choice between two things or activities with prefer ... to and like ... more than.

### Important rules
- Use prefer A to B: I prefer tea to soda.
- Use like A more than B: I like tea more than soda.
- After prefer, -ing forms can compare activities: I prefer reading to watching television.

### Examples
1. I prefer matooke to rice.
2. She likes football more than netball.
3. We prefer walking to cycling.
4. Amina likes mangoes more than oranges.
5. My uncle prefers tea to coffee.
6. He prefers drawing to singing.

### Worked example
**Question:** Rewrite using prefer: I like football more than netball.

**Answer:** I prefer football to netball.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I prefer tea than soda. | I prefer tea to soda. |
| She likes apples to oranges more. | She likes apples more than oranges. |
| We prefer walking than running. | We prefer walking to running. |
| He prefer rice to cassava. | He prefers rice to cassava. |
