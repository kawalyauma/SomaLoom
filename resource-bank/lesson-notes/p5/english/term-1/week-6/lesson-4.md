## WHILE AND WHEN

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of while and when.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
While and when connect events in the past. They can help us show actions happening together or interruptions.

### Important rules
- While often introduces an action that was continuing in the past.
- When often introduces a shorter event that interrupted a continuing action.
- Two long actions may happen at the same time: While Asha was reading, Musa was writing.

### Examples
1. While I was reading, the bell rang.
2. Amina was cooking when the visitors arrived.
3. While the boys were playing, the girls were singing.
4. The lights went off when we were studying.
5. While the teacher was speaking, we were listening.
6. When it started raining, we ran inside.

### Worked example
**Question:** Join: I was walking home. It started raining. (when)

**Answer:** I was walking home when it started raining.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| While the bell rang, I was writing. | When the bell rang, I was writing. |
| I was sleep when the rain began. | I was sleeping when the rain began. |
| While she reading, he was writing. | While she was reading, he was writing. |
| They was playing when it rained. | They were playing when it rained. |
