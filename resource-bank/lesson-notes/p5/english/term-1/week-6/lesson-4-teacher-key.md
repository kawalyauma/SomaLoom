### D. Short responses
15. Example: While often introduces an action that was continuing in the past.
16. Example: While I was reading, the bell rang.
17. Example: While and when connect events in the past. They can help us show actions happening together or interruptions.

### E. Original sentences
18. Example: While I was reading, the bell rang.
19. Example: Amina was cooking when the visitors arrived.
20. Example: While the boys were playing, the girls were singing.

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
