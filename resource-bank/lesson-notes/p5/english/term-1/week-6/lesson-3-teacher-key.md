# TEACHER ANSWER KEY
**Primary Five English Grammar — Term One, Week 6, Lesson 3: PREFER ... TO AND LIKE ... MORE THAN**

### A. Complete
1. to
2. than
3. to
4. than
5. to
6. than

### B. Rewrite or correct
7. I prefer football to netball.
8. I like mangoes more than oranges.
9. She prefers beans to rice.
10. They like tea more than juice.

### C. Correct the mistakes
11. I prefer tea to soda.
12. She likes apples more than oranges.
13. We prefer walking to running.
14. He prefers rice to cassava.

### D. Short responses
15. Example: Use prefer A to B: I prefer tea to soda.
16. Example: I prefer matooke to rice.
17. Example: We can express a choice between two things or activities with prefer ... to and like ... more than.

### E. Original sentences
18. Example: I prefer matooke to rice.
19. Example: She likes football more than netball.
20. Example: We prefer walking to cycling.

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
