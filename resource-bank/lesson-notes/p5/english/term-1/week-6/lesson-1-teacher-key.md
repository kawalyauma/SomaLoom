# TEACHER ANSWER KEY
**Primary Five English Grammar — Term One, Week 6, Lesson 1: THE PRESENT SIMPLE PASSIVE**

### A. Complete
1. is locked
2. are cleaned
3. are delivered
4. is swept
5. are prepared
6. is checked

### B. Rewrite or correct
7. Lunch is prepared by the cook every day.
8. The windows are cleaned by the pupils.
9. The doors are not opened at six.
10. Is the room swept daily?

### C. Correct the mistakes
11. The gate is locked.
12. The desks are polished.
13. The room is swept.
14. The food is cooked.

### D. Short responses
15. Example: Form: subject + am/is/are + past participle.
16. Example: The classroom is swept daily.
17. Example: The present simple passive is used for regular actions and facts when the receiver is more important than the doer.

### E. Original sentences
18. Example: The classroom is swept daily.
19. Example: The desks are cleaned every Friday.
20. Example: English is spoken in our school.

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
