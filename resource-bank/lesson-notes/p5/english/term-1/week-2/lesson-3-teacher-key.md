# TEACHER ANSWER KEY
**Primary Five English Grammar — Term One, Week Two, Lesson Three**

## Present Perfect Passive

### A. Complete the sentences
1. has been swept
2. have been sold
3. has been raised
4. have been written
5. has been repaired
6. have been washed
7. has been cooked
8. have been fed

### B. Active to passive
9. The library has been opened by the head teacher.
10. The trees have been planted by the pupils.
11. The window has been broken by Musa.
12. Lunch has been prepared by the cook.
13. The beans have been harvested by the farmers.
14. The coins have been counted by the shopkeeper.

### C. Correct the mistakes
15. The desks have been arranged.
16. The book has been written.
17. The compound has been swept.
18. The mangoes have been picked.
19. The door has been opened by the guard.
20. The balls have been taken away.

### D. Change form
21. The water has not been boiled.
22. Have the bags been packed?
23. Has the wall been painted?
24. The papers have been collected.

### E. Original sentences
25. Example: The board has been cleaned.
26. Example: The shoes have been polished.

Accept other grammatically correct present perfect passive sentences. For conversions, allow omission of the *by* phrase where the doer is not important, provided the meaning remains correct. Do not accept active constructions or missing *been*.
