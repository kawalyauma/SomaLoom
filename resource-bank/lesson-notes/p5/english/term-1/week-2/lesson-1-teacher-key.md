## Exercise E — sample answers
23. I **bought** a pencil yesterday.  
24. She **has taken** her books to school.  
25. We **have seen** the new library.  
26. By the time the teacher arrived, I **had written** the date.

Accept other grammatically correct original sentences that use the requested verb form. For 26, the past-perfect action should take place before a past reference point.

## Teaching notes
- Read the three forms aloud, especially *read–read–read* (past forms pronounced like *red*).
- Contrast *saw* with *seen*, *went* with *gone*, and *wrote* with *written*.
- Explain that *did not* takes the base form, as in *did not take*.
- For question 18, *teach–taught–taught* is an additional irregular verb introduced in practice; demonstrate its forms before assigning the exercise.
- Suggested marking: Questions 1–22: 1 mark each; questions 23–26: 2 marks each (correct target verb and meaningful sentence), total 30 marks.
