# TEACHER ANSWER KEY
**Primary Five English Grammar — Term One, Week Two, Lesson Four**

## First Conditional (IF1)

### A. Choose the correct verb
1. rains
2. works
3. will arrive
4. will look
5. finds
6. will celebrate

### B. Complete the sentences (examples)
7. If the classroom is dirty, we will sweep it.
8. If I finish my homework, I will play football.
9. If the teacher asks a question, I will raise my hand.
10. If our water tank is empty, we will fetch water from an approved safe source.

### C. Join using IF1
11. If it rains, we will carry umbrellas.
12. If you study carefully, you will understand the topic.
13. If we hurry, we will catch the bus.
14. If the pupils practise, they will sing better.

### D. Correct the mistakes
15. If I see Asha, I will tell her.
16. If she comes early, she will help us.
17. If the bell rings, we will enter the classroom.
18. I will carry my sweater if it is cold.
19. If he finishes his work, he will go home.
20. If they do not water the seedlings, the plants will dry.

### E. Change clause order
21. The pupils will understand if they listen carefully.
22. If we keep the classroom clean, the teacher will be pleased.
23. I will buy an exercise book if I have enough money.
24. If there is a storm, we will stay at home.

### F. Original sentences (examples)
25. If I wake up early, I will arrive at school on time.
26. If I finish my chores, I will help my younger sister.

**Marking note:** For open-ended items, accept any meaningful sentence with a present-simple if-clause and a will + base-verb result. Accept ordinary contractions. Require appropriate subject-verb agreement and punctuation.
