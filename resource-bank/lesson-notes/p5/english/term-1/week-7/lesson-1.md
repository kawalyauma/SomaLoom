## REVISION: AFFIRMATIVE, NEGATIVE AND INTERROGATIVE SENTENCES

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of revision: affirmative, negative and interrogative sentences.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
A sentence can state something, deny something or ask a question.

### Important rules
- An affirmative sentence states that something is true or happens: She reads.
- A negative sentence says it does not happen: She does not read.
- An interrogative sentence asks a question: Does she read? End it with a question mark.

### Examples
1. The pupils sing well.
2. The pupils do not sing loudly.
3. Do the pupils sing every day?
4. Musa was running.
5. Musa was not running.
6. Was Musa running?

### Worked example
**Question:** Make negative: He goes to school.

**Answer:** He does not go to school.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| Does she sings? | Does she sing? |
| He not is here. | He is not here. |
| Did they went? | Did they go? |
| You are ready. (question) Are you ready. | Are you ready? |
