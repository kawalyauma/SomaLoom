## THE POSITION OF ADVERBS IN A SENTENCE

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the position of adverbs in a sentence.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
An adverb of manner tells us how an action is done. Its position should make the meaning clear.

### Important rules
- An adverb of manner often comes after the verb if there is no object: She sings beautifully.
- When there is an object, the adverb often comes after it: He carried the bag carefully.
- Do not normally place an adverb of manner between a verb and its direct object: He read the letter carefully.

### Examples
1. The baby slept peacefully.
2. The children worked quietly.
3. Asha closed the door gently.
4. They swept the classroom carefully.
5. Musa answered the question correctly.
6. The crowd cheered loudly.

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: She held gently the cup.

**Answer:** She held the cup gently.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| He read carefully the letter. | He read the letter carefully. |
| She closed gently the gate. | She closed the gate gently. |
| They washed quickly the plates. | They washed the plates quickly. |
| We carried carefully the table. | We carried the table carefully. |
