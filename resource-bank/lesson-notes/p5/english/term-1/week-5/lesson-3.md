## CLASSES OF ADVERBS

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of classes of adverbs.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
Adverbs can be grouped according to what they tell us about an action.

### Important rules
- Manner tells how: carefully, slowly, well.
- Time tells when: yesterday, soon, today.
- Place tells where: here, outside, upstairs.
- Frequency tells how often: always, often, sometimes. Duration tells how long: briefly, all day.

### Examples
1. She speaks softly. (manner)
2. We shall meet tomorrow. (time)
3. Come here. (place)
4. He always washes his hands. (frequency)
5. They waited all day. (duration)
6. The children played outside. (place)

### Worked example
**Question:** Classify: slowly

**Answer:** manner

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| quickly — time | quickly — manner |
| yesterday — place | yesterday — time |
| always — manner | always — frequency |
| here — duration | here — place |
