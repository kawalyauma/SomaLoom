## QUESTION TAGS

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of question tags.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
A question tag is a short question added at the end of a statement to ask for confirmation.

### Important rules
- A positive statement usually takes a negative tag: She is here, isn’t she?
- A negative statement usually takes a positive tag: He is not late, is he?
- Use the same helping verb when possible; if there is none in the present or past simple, use do, does or did.

### Examples
1. You are ready, aren’t you?
2. He is not sleepy, is he?
3. Amina sings well, doesn’t she?
4. They played yesterday, didn’t they?
5. We have finished, haven’t we?
6. The pupils cannot swim, can they?

### Worked example
**Question:** Add tag: The bell rang.

**Answer:** The bell rang, didn’t it?

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| She is here, is she? | She is here, isn’t she? |
| He does not know, doesn’t he? | He does not know, does he? |
| We went home, don’t we? | We went home, didn’t we? |
| They can sing, cannot they? | They can sing, can’t they? |
