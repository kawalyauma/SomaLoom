## FORMATION OF ADVERBS

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of formation of adverbs.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
An adverb adds information about an action, often telling us how it happens.

### Important rules
- Many adverbs of manner are formed by adding -ly: quick → quickly.
- When an adjective ends in -y, change y to i and add -ly: happy → happily.
- Some adverbs do not end in -ly: fast, hard, well.

### Examples
1. The children spoke politely.
2. Amina worked carefully.
3. Musa ran quickly.
4. They sang happily.
5. The teacher explained clearly.
6. She plays well.

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: She sings beautiful.

**Answer:** She sings beautifully.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| happyly | happily |
| easyly | easily |
| goodly | well |
| fastly | fast |
