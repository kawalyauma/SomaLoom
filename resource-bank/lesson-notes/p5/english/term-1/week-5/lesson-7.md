## SPECIAL FORMS OF QUESTION TAGS

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of special forms of question tags.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
A few expressions have special question tags that need careful attention.

### Important rules
- I am late, aren’t I? is the usual tag, not amn’t I.
- Let us (let’s) ... often takes shall we?
- A positive request with will often takes will you?
- Commands and requests often use will you?; negative commands may also take will you?

### Examples
1. I am your classmate, aren’t I?
2. Let’s clean the room, shall we?
3. Open the window, will you?
4. Don’t make noise, will you?
5. You will help, won’t you?
6. He used to play here, didn’t he?

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: I am correct, amn’t I?

**Answer:** I am correct, aren’t I?

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I am early, amn’t I? | I am early, aren’t I? |
| Let’s go, will we? | Let’s go, shall we? |
| Close the door, do you? | Close the door, will you? |
| He used to read, usedn’t he? | He used to read, didn’t he? |
