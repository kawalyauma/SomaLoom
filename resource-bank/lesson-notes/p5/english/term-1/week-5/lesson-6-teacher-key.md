# TEACHER ANSWER KEY
**Primary Five English Grammar — Term One, Week 5, Lesson 6: QUESTION TAGS**

### A. Complete
1. isn’t she
2. aren’t they
3. can he
4. didn’t you
5. haven’t we
6. doesn’t he

### B. Rewrite or correct
7. The bell rang, didn’t it?
8. The girls are ready, aren’t they?
9. He is ill, isn’t he?
10. Asha does not sing, does she?

### C. Correct the mistakes
11. She is here, isn’t she?
12. He does not know, does he?
13. We went home, didn’t we?
14. They can sing, can’t they?

### D. Short responses
15. Example: A positive statement usually takes a negative tag: She is here, isn’t she?
16. Example: You are ready, aren’t you?
17. Example: A question tag is a short question added at the end of a statement to ask for confirmation.

### E. Original sentences
18. Example: You are ready, aren’t you?
19. Example: He is not sleepy, is he?
20. Example: Amina sings well, doesn’t she?

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
