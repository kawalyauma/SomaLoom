## THE PRESENT SIMPLE TENSE

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the present simple tense.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
The present simple expresses habits, repeated actions and facts.

### Important rules
- Use the base form with I, you, we and they: we read.
- Add -s or -es with he, she and it: she reads, he watches.
- Use do/does for questions and do not/does not for negatives; the main verb stays in the base form.

### Examples
1. We study every day.
2. Asha walks to school.
3. The sun rises in the east.
4. Musa does not drink tea.
5. Do the pupils sing?
6. Does the teacher write clearly?

### Worked example
**Question:** Make negative: She likes mangoes.

**Answer:** She does not like mangoes.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| She walk home. | She walks home. |
| He does not walks. | He does not walk. |
| Does she reads? | Does she read? |
| They goes to school. | They go to school. |
