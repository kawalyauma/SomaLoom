## COMPARISONS OF ADVERBS

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of comparisons of adverbs.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
Some adverbs can show three degrees: positive, comparative and superlative.

### Important rules
- The positive degree makes no comparison: fast.
- The comparative degree compares two actions: faster.
- The superlative degree compares three or more: fastest.
- Longer adverbs may take more and most: carefully, more carefully, most carefully.

### Examples
1. Musa runs fast.
2. Asha runs faster than Musa.
3. Kato runs the fastest of the three.
4. She writes carefully.
5. Amina writes more carefully than John.
6. Of the group, Amina writes most carefully.

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: He runs more faster.

**Answer:** He runs faster.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| more faster | faster |
| most fastest | fastest |
| carefullier | more carefully |
| gooder | better |
