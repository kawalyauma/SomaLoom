# PRIMARY FIVE ENGLISH GRAMMAR

## MORE PRACTICE ON ADVERBS

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of more practice on adverbs.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
Adverbs give useful details about actions. Practise forming and using them correctly.

### Important rules
- Many manner adverbs end in -ly: slow → slowly.
- Keep irregular forms in mind: good → well; fast → fast.
- Place the adverb clearly, often after the object if one is present.

### Examples
1. Asha answered politely.
2. We crossed the road carefully.
3. The girl sang beautifully.
4. The driver stopped suddenly.
5. The class worked quietly.
6. The footballers played well.

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: The child spoke loud.

**Answer:** The child spoke loudly.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| slowley | slowly |
| he ran good | he ran well |
| happyly | happily |
| She carried gently the tray. | She carried the tray gently. |
