## RELATIVE PRONOUNS: WHO, WHOM, WHOSE, WHICH AND THAT

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of relative pronouns: who, whom, whose, which and that.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
Relative pronouns connect information about a noun to the rest of a sentence.

### Important rules
- Who refers to a person as the subject: the girl who sings.
- Whom refers to a person as the object, especially in formal writing.
- Whose shows possession; which refers to things and animals; that can refer to people or things in defining clauses.

### Examples
1. The girl who won is in P5.
2. This is the boy whom we met.
3. The farmer whose hoe broke went home.
4. This is the book which I borrowed.
5. The goat that escaped has returned.
6. The teacher who helped us is kind.

### Worked example
**Question:** Join: The boy is Musa. He won the race.

**Answer:** The boy who won the race is Musa.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| The boy which sings | the boy who sings |
| The girl who bag was lost | the girl whose bag was lost |
| The book whose I read | the book which I read |
| The goat who escaped | the goat that escaped |
