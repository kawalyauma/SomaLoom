# PRIMARY FIVE ENGLISH GRAMMAR
**Term One | Week 4 | Lesson 2**

## ARTICLES: A, AN AND THE (PART ONE)

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of articles: a, an and the (part one).
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
An article is a word placed before a noun. A and an introduce an unspecified singular countable noun.

### Important rules
- Use a before a consonant sound: a book, a uniform.
- Use an before a vowel sound: an apple, an hour.
- Choose the article by the sound, not simply the first letter.

### Examples
1. She has a pencil.
2. He ate an orange.
3. We saw a goat.
4. Amina is an honest pupil.
5. Our school has a uniform.
6. He waited for an hour.

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: She has a orange.

**Answer:** She has an orange.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| an goat | a goat |
| a egg | an egg |
| an uniform | a uniform |
| a hour | an hour |
