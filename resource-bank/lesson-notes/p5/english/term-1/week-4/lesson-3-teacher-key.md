### C. Correct the mistakes
11. I saw a cow. The cow was black.
12. She bought an egg. The egg broke.
13. Please open the door of our classroom.
14. Amina is here.

### D. Short responses
15. Example: Use the when both speaker and listener know which noun is meant.
16. Example: I saw a dog. The dog was brown.
17. Example: The points to a particular person or thing that is known or already mentioned.

### E. Original sentences
18. Example: I saw a dog. The dog was brown.
19. Example: Please close the classroom door.
20. Example: She bought an orange. The orange was sweet.

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
