# PRIMARY FIVE ENGLISH GRAMMAR
**Term One | Week 4 | Lesson 3**

## ARTICLES: A, AN AND THE (PART TWO)

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of articles: a, an and the (part two).
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
The points to a particular person or thing that is known or already mentioned.

### Important rules
- Use the when both speaker and listener know which noun is meant.
- Use a or an when mentioning one unspecified thing for the first time.
- Use the when referring again to that same thing.

### Examples
1. I saw a dog. The dog was brown.
2. Please close the classroom door.
3. She bought an orange. The orange was sweet.
4. The sun gives us light.
5. I found a pencil. The pencil belongs to Musa.
6. He is reading the book you lent him.

### Worked example
**Question:** Correct: A sun is shining.

**Answer:** The sun is shining.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I saw a cow. A cow was black. | I saw a cow. The cow was black. |
| She bought an egg. An egg broke. | She bought an egg. The egg broke. |
| Please open a door of our classroom. | Please open the door of our classroom. |
| The Amina is here. | Amina is here. |
