# PRIMARY FIVE ENGLISH GRAMMAR
**Term One | Week 4 | Lesson 1**

## USING MUST AND MUST NOT

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of using must and must not.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
Must expresses a strong duty or something necessary. Must not (mustn’t) says that an action is forbidden.

### Important rules
- Use must + base verb: We must wash our hands.
- Use must not + base verb: We must not litter.
- Must not does not mean the same as do not have to: must not forbids an action.

### Examples
1. Pupils must respect one another.
2. We must cross the road carefully.
3. You must not run across a busy road.
4. We must not waste clean water.
5. Must we wear our school uniform?
6. Visitors must sign the visitors’ book.

### Worked example
**Question:** Rewrite as a rule with must: It is necessary to wash hands.

**Answer:** You must wash your hands.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| You must to wash hands. | You must wash your hands. |
| We must not to litter. | We must not litter. |
| She must wears shoes. | She must wear shoes. |
| He must not throws stones. | He must not throw stones. |
