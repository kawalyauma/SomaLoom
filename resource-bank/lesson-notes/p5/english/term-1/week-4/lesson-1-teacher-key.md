### D. Short responses
15. Example: Use must + base verb: We must wash our hands.
16. Example: Pupils must respect one another.
17. Example: Must expresses a strong duty or something necessary. Must not (mustn’t) says that an action is forbidden.

### E. Original sentences

19. Example: We must cross the road carefully.
20. Example: You must not run across a busy road.

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
