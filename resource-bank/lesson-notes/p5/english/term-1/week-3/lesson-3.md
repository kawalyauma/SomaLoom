## THE USE OF SINCE, FOR AND FROM

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the use of since, for and from.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
Since, for and from help us talk about time. They have different meanings and uses.

### Important rules
- Since introduces a starting point: since Monday, since 2020.
- For introduces a length of time: for two hours, for three weeks.
- From introduces a starting time or place and often appears with to or until.

### Examples
1. We have lived here since 2022.
2. The lesson has lasted for forty minutes.
3. Our library opens from eight o’clock to four o’clock.
4. She has been ill since Tuesday.
5. They waited for half an hour.
6. We walked from school to the market.

### Worked example
**Question:** Choose the correct word: I have had this book since/for two weeks.

**Answer:** for

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I have waited since two hours. | I have waited for two hours. |
| She has lived here for 2020. | She has lived here since 2020. |
| We walk since home to school. | We walk from home to school. |
| The meeting is from Monday for Tuesday. | The meeting is from Monday to Tuesday. |
