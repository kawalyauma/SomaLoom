## THE PAST CONTINUOUS TENSE

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the past continuous tense.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
The past continuous describes an activity that was going on at a particular time in the past.

### Important rules
- Form: subject + was/were + verb-ing.
- Use was with I, he, she and it; use were with you, we and they.
- Use was/were not + verb-ing for negatives.

### Examples
1. I was reading at seven o’clock.
2. They were weeding the school garden.
3. The baby was sleeping.
4. We were singing during assembly.
5. Was she writing a letter?
6. The children were not shouting.

### Worked example
**Question:** Make negative: She was dancing.

**Answer:** She was not dancing.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| She were running. | She was running. |
| They was reading. | They were reading. |
| I was play football. | I was playing football. |
| Were he working? | Was he working? |
