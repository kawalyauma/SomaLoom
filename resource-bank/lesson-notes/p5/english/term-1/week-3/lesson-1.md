## THE PAST SIMPLE TENSE

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the past simple tense.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
The past simple tells us about actions that started and finished in the past.

### Important rules
- Regular past verbs often end in -ed: work → worked.
- Some verbs change completely: go → went; eat → ate.
- Use did not + base verb in negatives and Did + subject + base verb in questions.

### Examples
1. We visited our grandmother on Saturday.
2. Amina walked to school yesterday.
3. The boys played netball last week.
4. Moses ate matooke for lunch.
5. The teacher wrote on the board.
6. Did you see the match?

### Worked example
**Question:** Make negative: She cooked the beans.

**Answer:** She did not cook the beans.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I goed home. | I went home. |
| She did not went. | She did not go. |
| Did he played? | Did he play? |
| Yesterday I am late. | Yesterday I was late. |
