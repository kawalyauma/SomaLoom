### D. Short responses
15. Example: Form: receiving subject + was/were + past participle.
16. Example: The door was opened by Asha.
17. Example: The past simple passive describes a completed past action and focuses on the person or thing receiving it.

### E. Original sentences
18. Example: The door was opened by Asha.
19. Example: The desks were repaired yesterday.
20. Example: The beans were harvested last month.

Accept other grammatically correct equivalent answers. For open-ended questions, check that the sentence follows the rules of the lesson and expresses a clear meaning.
