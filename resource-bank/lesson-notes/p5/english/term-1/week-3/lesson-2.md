## THE PAST SIMPLE PASSIVE

### Learning objectives
- Explain the meaning of the past simple passive.
- Recognise and use the correct sentence patterns.
- Write clear and correct sentences in this lesson.

### Meaning
The past simple passive describes a completed past action and focuses on the person or thing receiving it.

### Important rules
- Form: receiving subject + was/were + past participle.
- Use was with a singular subject and were with a plural subject.
- A by-phrase may tell us who performed the action.

### Examples
1. The door was opened by Asha.
2. The desks were repaired yesterday.
3. The beans were harvested last month.
4. The gate was painted blue.
5. The room was cleaned by the pupils.
6. Were the books returned?

### Worked example
**Question:** Make passive: The gardener watered the flowers.

**Answer:** The flowers were watered by the gardener.

### Common mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| The plate were washed. | The plate was washed. |
| The chairs was broken. | The chairs were broken. |
| The wall was paint. | The wall was painted. |
| The maize was ate. | The maize was eaten. |
