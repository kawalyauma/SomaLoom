# PRIMARY SIX SCIENCE — TERM ONE

# Teacher answer key for expanded study notes

## 1. Mammals: corrected groups and examples

1. **Why are bats mammals?**

   They have hair and produce milk for their young.

2. **Give three examples of rodents.**

   Rats, mice and porcupines.

3. **Why is it incorrect to put seals under cetaceans?**

   Seals are pinnipeds; cetaceans include whales and dolphins.

4. **Name one monotreme and one marsupial.**

   Platypus or echidna; kangaroo or koala.


---

## 2. Birds, reptiles, fish and amphibians

1. **What feature identifies a living bird?**

   Feathers.

2. **Give two examples of reptiles.**

   Snake, lizard, tortoise or crocodile.

3. **Which organs do fish use to breathe?**

   Gills.

4. **Describe one change during frog metamorphosis.**

   Tadpole develops legs and lungs and loses its tail as it becomes an adult frog.


---

## 3. Arthropods, molluscs and worms

1. **Identify the three body regions of a housefly.**

   Head, thorax, abdomen.

2. **How do insects differ from spiders in number of legs?**

   Six legs versus eight legs.

3. **What is the benefit of earthworms in soil?**

   They loosen and aerate soil.

4. **Give two molluscs.**

   Snail and slug.


---

## 4. Animal care and conservation

1. **List three ways to care for a cow.**

   Suitable feed, clean water, shelter, hygiene or veterinary care.

2. **State two reasons to protect wild animals.**

   Pollination, seed dispersal, food webs or biodiversity.


---

## 5. Sound: production and properties

1. **What causes sound?**

   Vibration.

2. **Distinguish pitch and loudness.**

   Pitch depends on vibration frequency; loudness on amplitude.

3. **What is an echo?**

   Reflected sound heard again.

4. **Can sound travel in a vacuum?**

   No, it needs a material medium.


---

## 6. Sound in living things, instruments and recording

1. **Which part produces voiced sound in humans?**

   Vocal folds.

2. **Name a percussion and string instrument.**

   Drum or xylophone; guitar or violin.

3. **What does a microphone do?**

   Converts sound vibrations into an electrical signal.

4. **State one use of recorded sound.**

   Evidence, learning, research, entertainment or communication.


---

## 7. Structure, function and care of the ear

1. **What is the function of the cochlea?**

   Converts vibrations into nerve signals using sensory cells.

2. **Trace the sound pathway from pinna to brain.**

   Pinna, ear canal, eardrum, middle-ear bones, cochlea, auditory nerve, brain.

3. **What helps the body maintain balance?**

   Semicircular canals.

4. **Give two ways to protect hearing.**

   Reduce loud noise; keep objects out of ears; seek care when symptoms occur.


---

## 8. Circulatory system and heart anatomy

1. **What are the three components of the circulatory system?**

   Heart, blood and blood vessels.

2. **Name the four chambers.**

   Right and left atria, right and left ventricles.

3. **What do valves do?**

   Prevent backward blood flow.

4. **Why is the pulmonary artery unusual?**

   It carries oxygen-poor blood away from the heart to lungs.


---

## 9. Blood vessels, blood components and circulation health

1. **Explain the difference between arteries and veins.**

   Arteries carry blood away; veins towards heart.

2. **What do red and white blood cells do?**

   Red cells carry oxygen; white cells fight infections.

3. **What is the function of platelets?**

   Help clotting.

4. **Is sickle-cell disease caught through contact?**

   No, it is inherited.


---

## 10. HIV, prevention and blood safety

1. **What does HIV stand for?**

   Human immunodeficiency virus.

2. **Can HIV spread through a handshake?**

   No.

3. **Give two safe measures against blood-borne infections.**

   Do not share needles/razors; ensure safe screened transfusions; use protective first aid measures.

4. **Why should people not judge HIV status by appearance?**

   A person living with HIV can look well.


---

## 11. Alcohol, smoking, essential and traditional medicines

1. **How does fermentation produce alcohol?**

   Yeast converts sugars into alcohol and carbon dioxide.

2. **Distinguish active from passive smoking.**

   Active means smoking; passive means breathing another person's smoke.

3. **What is an essential medicine?**

   Medicine selected to meet priority health needs.

4. **Give two dangers of medicine misuse.**

   Poisoning, side effects, dependence, harmful interactions or ineffective treatment.

5. **What should a pupil do when offered an unknown tablet?**

   Refuse and tell a trusted adult.
