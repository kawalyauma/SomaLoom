# PRIMARY SIX SOCIAL STUDIES — LOCAL TIME AND TIME ZONES

## Teacher answer key

### Content checks

- Longitude calculation: 360° ÷ 24 hours = 15° per hour; 60 minutes ÷ 15° = 4 minutes per degree.
- Solar-time examples use the idealised longitude rule, not legal national time-zone boundaries.
- Uganda standard clock time is EAT (UTC+3) all year.

### Exercise answers

**1. What is meant by local solar time?**
Answer: Time based on the position of the Sun at a particular place; solar noon is when the Sun is highest.

**2. What is a time zone?**
Answer: A region that uses the same agreed standard clock time.

**3. What do lines of longitude measure?**
Answer: How far east or west a place lies from the Prime Meridian (0°).

**4. In which direction does Earth rotate?**
Answer: From west to east.

**5. How many hours of idealised solar-time difference are represented by 30° of longitude?**
Answer: 30° ÷ 15° per hour = 2 hours.

**6. If it is 09:00 at 0°, what is the idealised solar time at 45°E? Show the steps.**
Answer: 45° ÷ 15° = 3 hours; east is ahead; 09:00 + 3 hours = 12:00.

**7. If it is 13:00 at 0°, what is the idealised solar time at 15°W? Show the steps.**
Answer: 15° ÷ 15° = 1 hour; west is behind; 13:00 − 1 hour = 12:00.

**8. If it is 06:00 UTC, what is the official time in Uganda?**
Answer: 06:00 + 3 hours = 09:00 EAT.

**9. Why do people across Uganda use the same official clock time?**
Answer: Uganda follows one agreed national standard time, East Africa Time (UTC+3).

**10. Give one difference between local solar time and standard time.**
Answer: Solar time follows the Sun’s position at a longitude; standard time is the agreed official clock time.

**11. Is every real time zone exactly 15° wide? Explain.**
Answer: No. Real boundaries are set for practical and administrative reasons; they are not always straight or 15° wide.

**12. Town A is at 60°E and Town B at 15°E. Which has later solar time, and by how many hours?**
Answer: 60°E − 15°E = 45°; 45° ÷ 15° = 3 hours; Town A is 3 hours ahead in idealised solar time.

### Marking guidance
Accept correct equivalent wording. For time calculations, award credit for the longitude difference, conversion to hours and correct east/west direction. Do not accept an answer obtained only from the product or a formula when its assumptions are false.
