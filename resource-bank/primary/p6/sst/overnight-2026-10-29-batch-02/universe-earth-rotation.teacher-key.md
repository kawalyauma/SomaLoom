# PRIMARY SIX SOCIAL STUDIES — THE UNIVERSE AND EARTH’S ROTATION

## Teacher answer key

### Content checks

- Eight main planets; Pluto is a dwarf planet.
- One solar day is about 24 hours; one orbit of the Sun is about 365¼ days.
- Earth’s rotation causes day and night; its tilted axis plus revolution explains seasons.

### Exercise answers

**1. What is the universe?**
Answer: All space and everything in it, including stars, planets, galaxies and other matter and energy.

**2. What is a star? Give one example.**
Answer: A huge, hot body of gas that produces its own light; example: the Sun.

**3. What is a planet?**
Answer: A large, nearly round body that travels around a star and reflects light.

**4. What is a natural satellite? Give an example.**
Answer: A natural object that travels around a planet; example: Earth’s Moon.

**5. Name the galaxy that contains our Solar System.**
Answer: The Milky Way.

**6. Write the eight planets in order from the Sun.**
Answer: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.

**7. Which planet is third from the Sun?**
Answer: Earth.

**8. Name the planets immediately before and after Earth in order from the Sun.**
Answer: Venus comes before Earth and Mars comes after Earth.

**9. What is Earth’s rotation?**
Answer: Earth spinning about its axis, from west to east.

**10. Explain why a place on Earth experiences day and night.**
Answer: As Earth turns, a place faces the Sun (day) and later turns away (night).

**11. About how long is one cycle of day and night on Earth?**
Answer: About 24 hours for the usual day–night cycle.

**12. What is Earth’s revolution, and about how long does it take?**
Answer: Earth travelling around the Sun; about 365¼ days, or one year.

**13. Is Pluto one of the eight main planets? Give its classification.**
Answer: No; Pluto is a dwarf planet.

**14. Give one safety rule for observing the sky.**
Answer: Never look directly at the Sun; accept another sensible safe-observation rule.

### Marking guidance
Accept correct equivalent wording and spelling. Check that the Sun is identified as a star, that Pluto is not counted as a main planet, and that rotation is not confused with revolution.
